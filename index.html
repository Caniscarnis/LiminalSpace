    Liminal spaces are often used in horror media to create a sense of unease and discomfort. The emptiness and surrealism of these spaces can evoke feelings of isolation and fear, making them effective settings for horror stories and games. The Backrooms, for example, is a popular horror concept that utilizes liminal spaces to create a terrifying and disorienting experience for players.
